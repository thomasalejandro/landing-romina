About Romina

Where legal rigor meets market instinct.

Before becoming an agent, Romina worked as a real estate paralegal. That foundation lets her anticipate risk, read every clause with care and negotiate complex transactions with confidence. Since 2016 she has guided families and investors across Nassau and Suffolk — and today advises on transactions throughout greater New York.

Fully bilingual in English and Spanish, Romina delivers a discreet, transparent experience to American and Latino clients alike.
